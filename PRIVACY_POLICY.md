# Get In Coffee Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 25, 2026**

Get In Coffee ("we," "our," or "us") provides a coffee ordering app for browsing products and branches, placing delivery or pickup orders, and managing an account. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Get In Coffee customer app.

## Information we handle

Depending on the features you use, the app and our services may handle:

- **Account information:** your name, phone number and country calling code, account credentials, and profile photo if you add one.
- **Orders and preferences:** products and quantities, selected branch, delivery or pickup choice, order status and history, coupon use, favorites, and points or stars activity.
- **Delivery information:** saved address details, recipient name and phone number, delivery instructions, and address coordinates. If you grant location permission, the app may use your device location to help find nearby branches or select a delivery point. You can manage location permission in your device settings.
- **Payment information:** payment choice and payment-method references needed to process an order. Card payments are handled by Stripe. We do not ask you to enter your full card number into our own forms; Stripe may process card details under its own privacy policy.
- **Notifications:** a Firebase Cloud Messaging device token and notification preferences needed to send order updates and other app notifications. You can disable notifications in your device settings.
- **Diagnostics:** crash reports and related technical information sent through Firebase Crashlytics to help us identify and fix app failures.

## How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; display nearby branches and available products; save addresses and preferences; accept, process, and deliver orders; process payments; apply coupons and rewards; send order and service notifications; respond to support requests; and diagnose, maintain, and protect the app and its services.

## Service providers and sharing

We share information only as needed to provide the app and its services, including with:

- **Our backend service providers**, which host and operate account, menu, address, order, and support functions.
- **Google Maps services**, which provide maps, place search, and address lookup features. Location or search information may be sent to Google when you use those features.
- **Stripe**, which processes card payments and related payment information.
- **Firebase by Google**, which supports push notifications and crash diagnostics.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use personal information for cross-app targeted advertising. Service providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies as well as our instructions where applicable.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transfer. We will update this policy if our practices change.

## Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain order and account records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. You can request account deletion from the **Delete Account** option in the app's profile menu. Deletion requests are sent to our service for processing. Some records may be retained where required by law or needed to resolve outstanding transactions or disputes.

## Your choices

You can choose whether to grant location permission, allow notifications, add a profile photo, save addresses, or save a payment method. Some app features may not work without the relevant permission or information. You can also contact support using the **Support** option in the app.

## Children's privacy

Get In Coffee is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us through the in-app Support option so we can review the request.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was most recently revised. Continued use of the app after an update means the updated policy applies to future use.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Get In Coffee through the **Support** option in the app's profile menu.
